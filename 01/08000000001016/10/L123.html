How this QR code works

It is a web address that carries GS1 codes. The path holds the product and the lot; the part after the question mark holds the date.

marcoruberto-gs1it.github.io/gs1-italy-epcis-videogame/01/08000000001016/10/L123?15=261130

  • www

    Domain marcoruberto-gs1it.github.io/gs1-italy-epcis-videogame

    The website of whoever makes or sells the product. Here it is the game's site: the brand chooses it, id.gs1.org is not needed.

  • (01)

    GTIN, the product code 08000000001016

    Global Trade Item Number: identifies the type of product, such as the yogurt or the milk. It is the same number as in the barcode.

  • (10)

    Batch/lot number L123

    The production lot number, such as L123. Pots made together share the same lot: it is the key data for withdrawals and recalls.

  • (15)

    Best before 261130

    The date in year, month, day format: 261130 means 30 November 2026.
